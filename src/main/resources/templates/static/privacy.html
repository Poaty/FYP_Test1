Privacy

This is an academic project, not a commercial platform. Plain-English summary of what is stored and why.

What we store

  • Your username, email, hashed password, and optional bio.
  • Posts you write and comments you make, with timestamps.
  • Movies you've written about, using metadata cached from OMDb.
  • Watch parties you create and RSVPs you make.

Where it's stored

A PostgreSQL database hosted on Supabase. Database connections use TLS. Passwords are stored as BCrypt hashes, not plain text.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party trackers.
  • No advertising.
  • No selling or licensing of user data.
  • No cookies beyond the session cookie used to keep you logged in.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Send you a copy of data linked to your account.
  • Delete your account and the posts, comments, RSVPs and watch parties linked to it.
  • Withdraw consent for any user-testing data collection you agreed to.

Email the project author at N1205202@my.ntu.ac.uk to make any of these requests. Responses within 7 days.

Current limitation

Account deletion is currently manual. A self-service delete-account button would be added in a production version.

Changes

Any material change to this policy will be communicated to active users by email before it takes effect.
